## Design Context

### Users
A personal dashboard for the owner of a Dokploy server to schedule Zoom meetings and reuse an interactive Google-linked Zoom login. The supplied brief includes registration-required meetings that open shortly before their start time.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, dependable. A clean light dashboard is the initial design assumption, pending user preference.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm paper background, ink typography, deep green actions, restrained borders. A schedule-first workspace with a separate browser and settings view. No invented meeting data or success statistics.

### Design Principles
- Show the next action and actual meeting state clearly.
- Distinguish a scheduled attempt from confirmed joining.
- Keep sign-in interruptions visible and recoverable.
- Make every action accessible by keyboard and usable on narrow screens.
- Keep configuration and setup guidance close to the feature it enables.
